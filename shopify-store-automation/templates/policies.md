# Policy and Page Templates — Shopify store

> Fill in every [bracketed] field with the owner's real facts before publishing.
> Never invent an address, timeframe, rate or promise. If the owner does not
> give a fact, leave the page unpublished and ask — do not guess.
> All six pages must be published and linked from the footer before launch.

## 1. About Us

**Purpose:** who the store is, what it sells, where it ships from.

**Outline:**
- One paragraph: [Store name] is an online store selling [what the store sells] in [market / country].
- One paragraph: how the store started / why it exists (owner's own words — do not invent a founding story).
- What customers can expect: [delivery promise, e.g. free delivery across Malaysia in 3–5 business days] and [payment options, e.g. cash on delivery and card payments].
- Contact line: email [store email] and phone / WhatsApp [store phone].

## 2. Contact

**Purpose:** how a customer reaches the store.

**Outline:**
- Store email: [store email]
- Phone / WhatsApp: [store phone]
- Shop address: [address — include only if the owner wants it public]
- Opening / dispatch hours: [e.g. Monday–Saturday, orders dispatched within 24 hours]
- Contact form (Shopify's built-in form).
- Note: "We reply within [timeframe the owner can actually meet, e.g. 24 hours]." Do not promise a faster reply than the owner can give.

## 3. Privacy Policy

**Purpose:** what customer data is collected and why.

**Outline:**
- What is collected: name, delivery address, phone number, email address, order details, payment details (handled by the payment provider — the store does not store card numbers).
- Why: to process and deliver orders, to contact the customer about their order, and (only with consent) to send order updates and review requests.
- Who it is shared with: the delivery / fulfilment partner (to deliver the order) and the payment provider (to take payment). No other sharing.
- How long it is kept: [owner's decision, e.g. as long as needed for orders, returns and accounts].
- Customer rights: the customer can ask what data the store holds about them and ask for it to be corrected or deleted — contact [store email].
- Cookies / analytics: [what the store actually uses, e.g. Shopify analytics and the store's ad pixel — list only what is really installed].

## 4. Refund / Return Policy

**Purpose:** the exact return window, condition and refund terms.

**Outline:**
- Return window: [number of days, e.g. 7 days] from delivery.
- Condition: product must be [owner's rule, e.g. unused, unopened, in original packaging].
- How to start a return: contact [store email / WhatsApp] with the order number.
- Return shipping: paid by [customer / store — owner's decision].
- Refund method and timing: refunded to the original payment method within [number of business days] after the returned product is received and checked.
- Non-returnable: [owner's rule, e.g. opened / used products, sale items — only if the owner says so].
- Damaged or wrong product: contact the store within [number of days] of delivery with a photo, and the store will [replacement / refund — owner's decision].

## 5. Shipping Policy

**Purpose:** zones, rates, delivery time and dispatch time — must match the store's shipping settings and the product-page trust block exactly.

**Outline:**
- Shipping zones: [e.g. across Malaysia].
- Rate: [e.g. Standard — FREE].
- Delivery time: [e.g. 3–5 business days].
- Dispatch: [e.g. orders dispatched within 24 hours; orders placed on a public holiday are dispatched the next day — include the holiday note only if the owner wants it].
- Tracking: the customer receives tracking details [by email / SMS — as the store actually sends them].
- Delivery problems: if a parcel is delayed or lost, contact [store email] with the order number.

## 6. Terms of Service

**Purpose:** ordering, payment, delivery, liability.

**Outline:**
- Ordering: placing an order is an offer to buy; the order is confirmed when the store confirms it [by email / message].
- Prices: prices are shown in [currency] and include / exclude tax as stated [owner's decision — must match the tax setting].
- Payment: accepted methods are [list only the methods actually enabled, e.g. cash on delivery and card payments].
- Delivery: delivery times are estimates; the store is not liable for delays caused by the courier or by public holidays.
- Product information: product names, sizes and descriptions are given by the supplier; if a product arrives different from its listing, the customer should contact the store under the Refund / Return Policy.
- Liability: the store's liability is limited to the price paid for the order.
- Governing law: [owner's market / country, e.g. the laws of Malaysia].
- Changes: these terms may be updated; the version at the time of ordering applies.
